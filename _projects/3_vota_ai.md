Vota AI is an AI assistant that helps citizens explore and compare the electoral proposals of Portuguese political parties, using retrieval-augmented generation over the official party programmes. It was launched for the Portuguese general elections in May 2025.

It was built at LIACC, University of Porto, under the supervision of Prof. Luís Paulo Reis and Prof. André Restivo. It reached more than 50,000 users and was featured in national media and on television.
