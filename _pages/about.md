I'm a Research Assistant at **INESC-ID** in Lisbon, working on **QSHIELD**, a joint INESC-ID / CMU project funded by FCT, with Prof. José Fragoso Santos (INESC-ID) and Prof. Limin Jia (CMU).

I hold an **MSc in Data Science from University College London**, where I graduated with Distinction. My MSc thesis, supervised by Prof. Ingemar Cox, studied how to combine LLM and human relevance judgments to evaluate the best retrieval systems; a paper based on it has been submitted to ECIR 2027. Before that, I studied **Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at the University of Porto**, where I was the best student of the bachelor's degree, with the highest GPA in the programme's history.

My research interests lie in understanding what reinforcement learning (RL) teaches LLMs during post-training. In particular, I am interested in:

- open-ended and long-horizon environments, where sparse rewards make training difficult;
- the capabilities that RL with verifiable rewards (RLVR) produces, and whether they transfer to other settings;
- reward hacking, and measuring when a policy starts optimizing the judge instead of the task.

My main research question is how to extend RL post-training beyond verifiable tasks, to open-ended and long-horizon problems. One of my long-term goals is to develop systems capable of open-ended scientific discovery.

Outside research, I co-founded **Miles AI**, a startup backed by Sterling Road VC that builds LLM and retrieval systems for drafting replies to customer emails, and co-developed **Vota AI**, a chatbot on party programmes for the Portuguese general elections used by more than 50,000 people.
